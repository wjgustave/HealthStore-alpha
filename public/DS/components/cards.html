Cards & surfaces

Flat, bordered, square white panels are the dominant container in the product (NHS aesthetic — no drop shadow). .hs-surface-card / .hs-surface-card-sm and the interactive .app-card own background, border and --radius-card (0px). Dashboard widgets use WidgetShell.
