Tabs

Tabs organise the product detail page into sections. The implementation follows NHS/GOV.UK tab structure with full keyboard support and a clear selected state. One of the strongest accessibility patterns in the product.

Component
PdpTabs / PdpTab
Classes
.nhsuk-tabs*

Example

Click a tab, or focus one and use the left/right arrow keys.

  • Overview
  • Evidence
  • Deployment & adoption
  • Commercial

Overview

Why it matters locally, context of use, and the one-line value proposition.

Evidence

Clinical evidence, case studies and NICE guidance references.

Deployment & adoption

Where it's live, scale and maturity, and what it takes locally.

Commercial

Indicative cost, procurement routes, tariff considerations and related funding.

States

  • Selected — white panel-connected tab, blue top border, bold label.
  • Inactive — grey #f0f4f5 background, regular weight (GOV.UK-style treatment).
  • Hover — underline with thicker decoration.
  • Focus — yellow outline + inset blue ring.

Responsive

Tabs wrap on small screens and become equal-width on a single row at md and above.

Accessibility

  • role="tablist" / role="tab" / role="tabpanel" with aria-selected.
  • Left/Right arrow keys move between tabs (roving focus); the active panel is revealed.
  • Inactive panels are hidden via .nhsuk-tabs__panel--hidden.
