Toggles & segmented controls

Controls that switch between mutually exclusive views or filter options. Most live on the comparison tool and are styled via dedicated .hs-compare-* classes.

Add-to-compare toggle

The catalogue / PDP control that adds a DTx app to the comparison basket. It is a pressed aria-pressed toggle whose label changes with state so it’s never ambiguous (CNT-02): “Add to compare” → “Added” (with a check). When the basket is full or the condition differs it becomes aria-disabled with a reason, staying in the tab order.

Lens control (segmented options)

A task-focused filter set rendered as a fieldset of selectable options.
