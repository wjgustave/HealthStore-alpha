Behaviour & accessibility

  • Change actions carry a visually hidden suffix so screen reader users know what they change.
  • Rows without an action use nhsuk-summary-list__row--no-actions (e.g. Product).
  • Empty optional answers read "Not provided" rather than being blank.
  • In-page journeys render Change as a <button class="hs-link-button"> (NHS link styling on a button element).
