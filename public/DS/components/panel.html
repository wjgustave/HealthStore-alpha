Confirmation panel

The NHS nhsuk-panel (the GOV.UK confirmation panel, NHS-branded in green). Used on the final page of a transaction to confirm success — for example when an expression of interest has been received. Follow it with a "What happens next" section.

Content & accessibility

  • Only use on confirmation pages, once per journey.
  • Keep the title short; put reference numbers or next steps in the body or below the panel.
  • Pair with a "What happens next" heading and onward links (e.g. View the EOI record).
  • Panel title is the page <h1> and receives focus when the step renders.
