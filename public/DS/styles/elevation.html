Elevation & radius

The NHS Design System is flat and square. Card surfaces use 0px corners (--radius-card). Other components (buttons, inputs) use 4px (--radius-md). Surfaces have no drop shadow. Shadows are reserved for true overlays (modals, toasts, popovers).
