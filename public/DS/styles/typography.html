Page title class

Use the semantic .page-title-h1 utility (from globals.css) for in-app H1s.

Find the right digital health technology

<h1 class="page-title-h1">Find the right digital health technology</h1>

Do

  • Use scale tokens rather than arbitrary rem/px sizes.
  • Keep one H1 per page; nest H2/H3 logically.

Don't

  • Hardcode font sizes inline (common in current pages).
  • Mix heading weight conventions across sections.
