Patterns

Cookie consent

Analytics consent is requested via a banner and managed via the cookies policy page. The pattern mirrors the GOV.UK cookie banner: a clear choice, no pre-ticked options, and an easy way to change the decision later.

Consent banner

Cookies on HealthStore

We'd like to use analytics cookies to understand how you use the service and make improvements. We also use essential cookies to keep the service working.

View cookies

Confirmation

After a choice, a brief confirmation replaces the question, with a link to change settings.

You've accepted analytics cookies. You can change your cookie settings at any time.

Reset control

The cookies policy page offers a reset button (ResetCookieConsentButton) that clears the stored choice and reloads.

Consent policy. Opt-out: Hotjar runs while the banner is showing (undecided) and after the user accepts; it is withheld only when the user explicitly rejects. The banner copy, the cookies page and HotjarWhenConsented document this behaviour.
