5. Economics — its own reconcile-heavy round (Round 5)

matt_demo’s “What it could be worth” renders the full commissioner_economics object: a headline (e.g. Luscii “£245k net for 500 patients”), net/cash/capacity/cost settings cards, a tariff & QOF note, and a funding-levers grid (emergency tariff, virtual ward tariff, QOF COPD007, MedTech Funding Mandate). None of this is on the page today — PdpLocalValue computes its own illustrative range instead.

Why it can’t just be ported.
  • Price conflict. The curated net figures embed £110/patient/year (500 × £110 = £55k cost) — the very price Round 1 suppressed as unverified.
  • Numeric conflict. “£245k net” contradicts the Round 2 illustrative range (−£65k to +£60k). Two different models on one page would undermine credibility.
  • Funding levers carry £ figures. Tariff amounts (£2,500–£5,500/admission, BPT £189) need a verification pass before they’re commissioner-facing.

Scope for Round 5 (to be a its own decision page): reconcile the price to a single source of truth; decide whether net figures publish at all or stay illustrative; split the non-numeric funding-levers / tariff-QOF narrative (shippable sooner) from the numeric settings (price-gated); and decide interactivity (ties to R4-1). Not shipped from this page.
