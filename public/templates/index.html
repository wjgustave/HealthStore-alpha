The 10 screens collapse into 8 templates. Of those, 5 are CMS content templates, (Home, Content page, Hub, Directory, Product detail page) the pages the Back Office team would create and edit as part of their general tasks. Current thinking is the Product detail page will be a hybrid of the set: a data-backed Wagtail page type whose product proposition and content narrative are CMS-edited alongside the structured product record. The remaining 3 are driven by product data or website functionality: the flows and tools stay in site code, with copy exposed as CMS-editable strings where the tags on each template page indicate.
